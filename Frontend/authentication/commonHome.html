ABOUT STATRICS

Two sides of the same dataset.

STATRICS exists because good statistics is scarce and good problems are everywhere. On one side are researchers, students, and businesses sitting on data they can't quite interrogate. On the other are statisticians who can — but need a place to be found.

We built a single home for both: a place to post a problem, claim a project, and get from raw numbers to a defensible answer, with the working shown, not hidden.

  • Verified statistical workers, reviewed by past clients
  • Transparent scope, timeline, and pricing before work begins
  • Every deliverable includes method notes, not just results
